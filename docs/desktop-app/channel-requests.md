# Channel Requests

[← Back to Documentation](./introduction.md)

In addition to opening channels with other nodes, it is possible to request inbound channels from an LSP in exchange for a certain percentage of the liquidity held by your node:

1. **Go to "Channels"**: Click on the "Channels" tab.
2. **Request New Channel**: Click "Order New Channel". ![Order New Channel](img/30_request_channel_step_0.png)
3. **Enter Peer Information**: Enter the URL of the LSP. ![Input LSP info](img/31_request_channel_step_1.png)
4. **Allocate Funds**: Specify the required channel capacity, liquidity of your channel, duration, and possibly RGB assets to be added. ![Channel settings](img/32_request_channel_step_2.png)
5. **Review Details and Pay**: Make sure all information is correct and proceed with the payment required by the LSP via LN or onchain. ![LN payment](img/33_request_channel_step_3_pay_LN.png) ![Onchain payment](img/34_request_channel_step_3_pay_onchain.png)
