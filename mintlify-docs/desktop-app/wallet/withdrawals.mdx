Withdrawing funds from your wallet allows you to send Bitcoin or RGB assets to other addresses, according to the following process:


1. **Open Withdraw**: Click **Withdraw** at the bottom of the left sidebar to open **Withdraw Funds**.
2. **Paste a destination** in **Address or Invoice**: a Bitcoin address, Lightning invoice, Lightning address or RGB invoice. The type is detected automatically.
3. **Enter Amount** (use **Max** for your full balance). Lightning amounts are capped by channel capacity.
4. **Fee Rate** (on-chain): **Slow**, **Normal**, **Fast** or **Custom**.
5. Click **Withdraw** (on-chain), **Pay Invoice** (Lightning) or **Send RGB Asset** (RGB).
6. **Confirm Withdrawal**: review the details and click **Confirm**.

**Differences between BTC and RGB on-chain withdrawal**: The steps to withdraw RGB assets are almost identical to those required to withdraw BTC, with the only difference being that you paste the recipient's RGB invoice instead of a Bitcoin address. Both blinded-UTXO and witness (address-based) invoice forms are supported, and the app detects the invoice type automatically when you paste it. For a witness RGB invoice you also set **Bitcoin Amount (sats) for Witness UTXO** (minimum 512 sats, 1,000-2,000 recommended). **Send as Gift/Donation** sends without receiver validation.
