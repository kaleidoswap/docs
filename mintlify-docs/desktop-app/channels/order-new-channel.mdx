This guide explains how to purchase a new Lightning Network channel with outbound liquidity from a KaleidoSwap LSP (Lightning Service Provider). This is particularly useful for users who want to quickly start trading new assets on the platform.

## Overview

The **Buy Channel** wizard has five steps:
1. **LSP Connection**.
2. **Configure Channel**.
3. **Channel Order**.
4. **Payment**.
5. **Complete**.

<FeatureVideo name="desktop-buy-usdt-channel" title="Buying a channel with USDT liquidity from the KaleidoSwap LSP" />

## Step 1: Connect to LSP

In the sidebar, open **Liquidity → Buy Channel**. Your account's LSP appears under **Connected LSP** (KaleidoSwap LSP on Mutinynet). To use another one, enter its URL under **Connect New LSP → LSP server URL** and click **Fetch LSP Info**. Then click **Configure Channel**.
<img src="/assets/images/desktop-app/12_connect_lsp.png" alt="Connecting to a Lightning Service Provider in the Desktop App" width={1412} height={702} />


## Step 2: Configure Channel Order

1. Set **Channel Capacity (sats)**: presets 100k/250k/500k/1M or the slider. The minimum is 50,000 sats; the maximum is the lower of 16,777,215 sats and the LSP's limit.
2. Set **Outbound Balance**, the amount **Available to send now** that you buy; the rest is what you **Can receive**. Example: For a 1M sat channel with 700k outbound, you'll have 300k inbound capacity.
3. Select how long the LSP needs to guarantee to keep the channel open (**channel duration**), among the following options:
- 1 month (4,320 blocks)
- 3 months (12,960 blocks)
- 6 months (25,920 blocks)
<img src="/assets/images/desktop-app/13_channel_order.png" alt="LSP channel order form with capacity options" width={1006} height={991} />

4. [Optional] Under **Add RGB Asset**, pick an asset (e.g. USDT) instead of **No Asset** and set the amount. You can also buy part of it upfront by setting the asset's **Available to send now**; the price comes from a live quote.

<img src="/assets/images/desktop-app/14_channel_order_assets.png" alt="Channel order form with RGB asset selection" width={952} height={866} />

Click **Review Details**, check the **Channel Order** summary, then click **Continue to Payment**.

## Step 3: Make Payment

To proceed with the channel opening, you will need to execute the payment. You will pay for:
- Your outbound liquidity, i.e. the funds that you will have immediately available to spend.
- The LSP service fee, which is different for each provider. Below you can find the fee structure of the default KaleidoSwap LSP.

### KaleidoSwap LSP Fee Structure
The form shows the LSP's live estimate (setup, capacity and duration fees); the figures below are an example.

When using KaleidoSwap LSP:
- Setup fee: 1,000 sats (one-time, per channel)
- Capacity fee: 1% of total channel capacity
- Duration fee: 1 sat per block of guaranteed channel lifetime
- The total fee is capped between a minimum of 2,000 sats and a maximum of 100,000 sats
- Example calculation:
  ```
  For a 1M sat channel locked for 1 month (4,320 blocks):
  - Setup fee: 1,000 sats.
  - Capacity fee: 10,000 sats (1% of 1M).
  - Duration fee: 4,320 sats (1 sat × 4,320 blocks).
  - Total fee: 15,320 sats.
  ```

The **Payment** step shows **Amount to Pay**, a **Payment expires in** countdown, and one **Unified payment QR** (on-chain + Lightning) with the on-chain address and Lightning invoice beside it. Pay from any external wallet, or click **Pay with Wallet Funds**, choose Lightning or on-chain (with a fee rate), and confirm with **Pay**. The amount covers your outbound balance, any asset bought upfront, and the LSP fees.
<img src="/assets/images/desktop-app/15_pay_the_channel.png" alt="Channel order payment screen with the unified payment QR" width={969} height={987} />

## Step 4: Channel Opening

After payment confirmation:
1. The LSP initiates channel opening and the order completes.
2. The channel appears in **Manage Channels** as **Pending** until its funding transaction confirms (the LSP advertises around 6 blocks, roughly an hour on mainnet).
3. Your channel becomes active with the specified:
   - Total capacity.
   - Outbound liquidity.
   - RGB assets (if requested).
   - Lock period.

## Order States

Your channel order can be in these states:
- `CREATED`: Order accepted by the LSP, waiting for payment and channel opening.
- `COMPLETED`: Channel successfully opened.
- `FAILED`: An error occurred during the process.

While an order is in the `CREATED` state, its payment moves through these sub-states:
- `EXPECT_PAYMENT`: Waiting for your payment.
- `HOLD`: Lightning payment received and held while the channel opens.
- `PAID`: Payment settled.
- `TIMEOUT` / `EXPIRED`: No payment arrived in time and the order lapsed.

## Refund Policy

If the order fails after payment, an on-chain refund is processed automatically to your wallet. If it has not arrived within 24 hours, contact the LSP with the **Order ID** shown on the failure screen (**Copy Order ID**).

## Best Practices

1. **Capacity Planning**
   - Calculate your required outbound/inbound ratio.
   - Consider future RGB asset needs.
   - Account for fees in your budget.

2. **During Order**
   - Keep the application open during payment.
   - Save your order ID.
   - Wait for all the required confirmations.

3. **After Opening**
   - Verify channel parameters.
   - Test with small transactions.
   - Monitor channel status.
