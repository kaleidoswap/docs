title: Withdraw from your wallet
description: "Withdraw Bitcoin from the KaleidoSwap Desktop App on-chain or via Lightning, with the steps, fees, and safety checks explained"
---

Withdrawing funds from your wallet allows you to send Bitcoin or RGB assets to other addresses, according to the following process:


1. **Open Withdraw**: Click the **"Withdraw"** quick action button in the left sidebar (also available from the **Quick Actions** menu). This opens the Withdraw modal.
2. **Withdrawal Method**: The withdrawal method is selected automatically when you paste a Bitcoin on-chain address, a Lightning invoice / address, or an RGB invoice.
3. **Enter Amount**: Specify the amount of Bitcoin or RGB asset to be withdrawn.
4. **Fee Rate**: slow, normal or fast for on-chain transaction.
5. **Review Transaction**: Double-check the details.
6. **Confirm and Send**: Click "Withdraw" to initiate the transaction.

**Differences between BTC and RGB on-chain withdrawal**: The steps to withdraw RGB assets are almost identical to those required to withdraw BTC, with the only difference being that you paste the recipient's RGB invoice instead of a Bitcoin address. Both blinded-UTXO and witness (address-based) invoice forms are supported, and the app detects the invoice type automatically when you paste it.
