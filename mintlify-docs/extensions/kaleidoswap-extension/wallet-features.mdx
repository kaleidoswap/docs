## Accounts

The KaleidoSwap Extension derives multiple protocol accounts from one recovery phrase:

| Account | What It Holds | Notes |
|---------|---------------|-------|
| **Spark** | Spark BTC, Spark-native assets, Lightning send/receive | Also powers Flashnet swaps and the cross-chain bridge |
| **Arkade** | Arkade BTC and Arkade-native assets | Boarding/offboarding and VTXO lifecycle; optional rotating-address (HD) mode |
| **Liquid** | L-BTC and Liquid assets such as USDt | Node-less and self-custodial; mainnet by default |
| **RGB-L1** | RGB assets and BTC on Bitcoin L1 | Node-less, runs in WebAssembly; mainnet by default |
| **RGB Lightning (RLN)** | RGB assets over Lightning channels | Optional, experimental; replaces the RGB-L1 backing when connected |

## Asset Management

<img src="/images/kaleidoswap-extension/08-dashboard.png" alt="KaleidoSwap dashboard" style={{ maxWidth: "360px", width: "100%", height: "auto" }} />

### Viewing Balances

The KaleidoSwap dashboard displays all your assets:

- **BTC balance** across Spark, Arkade, Liquid, Bitcoin L1, and RLN Lightning when connected
- **Spark assets** from the Spark account
- **Arkade assets** from the Arkade account
- **Liquid assets** from the Liquid account
- **RGB assets** from the node-less RGB-L1 account, or from RLN when connected
- **Protocol and network filters** for focusing on a single backing or network slice

Assets with the same ticker across backings are merged into a single dashboard row that expands into its per-network balances.

### Hiding Assets

You can hide assets you don't want on the dashboard from the manage-assets view. Hidden assets keep their balances; they are just removed from the list until you unhide them.

### Asset Details

Tap any asset to view details:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Ticker** | Short asset symbol (e.g., USDT) |
| **Name** | Full asset name |
| **Precision** | Decimal places |
| **Account** | Protocol account that owns the asset |
| **Spendable Balance** | Amount available for the selected route |
| **Network Badges** | Where the asset can be used, such as Spark, Arkade, Liquid, RGB-L1, or RGB-LN |

## Sending Payments

The KaleidoSwap Extension automatically detects the payment type based on what you paste or enter in the destination field.

### Supported Destination Formats

| Format | Detected As | Example |
|--------|------------|---------|
| **BOLT11 invoice** | Lightning payment | `lnbc1...` |
| **Lightning address** | Lightning payment | `name@example.com` |
| **LNURL-pay** | Lightning payment | `lnurl1...` |
| **RGB invoice** | RGB asset transfer | `rgb:...` |
| **Bitcoin address** | On-chain payment | `bc1q...`, `3...`, `1...` |
| **Liquid address / URI** | Liquid transfer | `lq1...`, `liquidnetwork:...` |
| **Spark address** | Spark transfer | Spark address string |
| **Arkade route** | Arkade transfer or offboard route | Arkade-compatible destination |

### Send Flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter Destination">
    Paste or type the destination address, invoice, or pubkey. The KaleidoSwap Extension auto-detects the payment type.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter Amount">
    For addresses and keysend, enter the amount in sats or BTC. For invoices, the amount is pre-filled from the invoice. **Max** fills the spendable balance and, for on-chain sends, reserves the network fee so the transaction can actually broadcast.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review">
    Review the payment details:
    - Destination
    - Amount
    - Selected route/account
    - Fee or route notes when available
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Send** to execute the payment. A status screen shows the result.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Receiving Payments

Generate invoices and addresses to receive supported assets across Spark, Arkade, Liquid, Bitcoin L1, Lightning, and RGB, depending on the selected asset and connected account.

<img src="/images/kaleidoswap-extension/09-deposit.png" alt="KaleidoSwap deposit flow" style={{ maxWidth: "360px", width: "100%", height: "auto" }} />

### Receive Tabs

Available receive methods depend on the selected asset and connected account:

| Route | What It Generates |
|-------|-------------------|
| **Spark** | Spark address or Spark Lightning invoice |
| **Arkade** | Arkade address, boarding address, or compatible receive route |
| **Liquid** | Liquid address for L-BTC or issued assets |
| **Bitcoin L1** | Bitcoin address for on-chain receive |
| **Lightning** | BOLT11 invoice where the selected account supports it |
| **RGB** | Blinded RGB invoice (node-less), or RGB Lightning invoice when RLN is connected |

### Receive Flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Asset">
    Choose BTC or another supported asset.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Route">
    Pick an available account route, such as Spark, Arkade, Liquid, Bitcoin L1, Lightning, or RGB.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Generate">
    Create the invoice or address. A QR code is displayed along with the text value.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Share">
    - **Copy** the invoice/address to clipboard
    - **Show QR** for the sender to scan
    - Lightning invoices show an **expiration countdown**
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Deposit and Withdraw

### Deposit

Deposit BTC or supported assets into the selected account:

1. Click **Deposit** on the dashboard
2. Choose the asset and route
3. Generate an address or invoice
4. Send funds from an external wallet or service

### Withdraw

Withdraw sends from the selected account to a detected destination:

1. Click **Withdraw** on the dashboard
2. Paste a Bitcoin address, Lightning invoice, Lightning address, LNURL, RGB invoice, Liquid address or `liquidnetwork:` URI, Spark address, or Arkade destination
3. Enter the amount
4. Review the suggested route and confirm

For withdrawals from Spark to external chains (EVM networks, Solana, and more), see [Bridge](/extensions/kaleidoswap-extension/bridge).

- Full transaction details

## KaleidoSwap Handle

You can claim a **Lightning address handle** (`yourname@kaleidoswap.me`) from Settings. The handle checks availability live as you type, links to your wallet for receiving Lightning payments, and is automatically re-linked when you restore the wallet.

## Full-Tab View

The wallet normally opens in the browser side panel (or a toolbar popup). For a larger workspace, use **Settings > Open in Full Tab** to open the wallet as a full browser tab.

## Settings

Access settings from the gear icon or bottom navigation.

<img src="/images/kaleidoswap-extension/11-settings.png" alt="KaleidoSwap settings" style={{ maxWidth: "360px", width: "100%", height: "auto" }} />

### Accounts

- **Spark** - Network selection, SDK details, and connection status
- **Arkade** - Network selection, VTXO settings, boarding flows, lifecycle settings, and an optional rotating-address (HD) mode with restore via gap scan
- **Liquid** - Network selection and the Esplora/waterfalls sync endpoint (a fast default is provided; you can supply your own)
- **RGB-L1** - Network selection plus the Esplora indexer and RGB proxy endpoints for the node-less account
- **RGB & Lightning** - Optional RLN account (direct URL or NWC), available through experimental settings

### Nostr

- **Nostr Account** - View your public key (npub), view/export the session private key when available, and re-derive or import your identity
- **Connected Sites** - Review every site and app connected to the wallet and revoke individual capabilities
- **Nostr Connect (NIP-46)** - Generate a `bunker://` connection string so external Nostr apps can request signatures remotely
- **Nostr Wallet Connect** - Pair external apps to the wallet over NWC with a QR code, and set amount-aware budgets

<Warning>
Never share your Nostr private key (nsec). Anyone with this key can sign events and act as you.
</Warning>

### Preferences

| Setting | Options |
|---------|---------|
| **Currency** | USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, CHF, CAD, CNY, KRW |
| **Language** | English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Japanese, Italian, Korean, Chinese |
| **Bitcoin units** | sats, BTC, mBTC, or fiat display modes |

### Security

- **Change Password** - Update your wallet password
- **Passkey Login** - Register or remove a passkey (WebAuthn) for unlocking the wallet
- **Backup Wallet** - Reveal the recovery phrase after password confirmation, publish an encrypted Nostr settings backup, and manage RGB backups (encrypted VSS cloud backup and local backup files for the node-less account, or an RGB node backup archive for RLN)
