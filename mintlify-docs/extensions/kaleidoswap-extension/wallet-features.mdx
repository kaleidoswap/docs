## Accounts

The KaleidoSwap Extension derives multiple protocol accounts from one recovery phrase:

| Account | What It Holds | Notes |
|---------|---------------|-------|
| **Spark** | Spark BTC, Spark-native assets, Lightning send/receive | Also powers Flashnet swaps and the cross-chain bridge |
| **Arkade** | Arkade BTC and Arkade-native assets | Boarding/offboarding and VTXO lifecycle; optional rotating-address (HD) mode |
| **Liquid** | L-BTC and Liquid assets such as USDt | Node-less and self-custodial; mainnet by default |
| **RGB-L1** | RGB assets and BTC on Bitcoin L1 | Node-less, runs in WebAssembly; mainnet by default |
| **RGB Lightning (RLN)** | RGB assets over Lightning channels | Optional, experimental; replaces the RGB-L1 backing when connected |
| **Bark** | BTC on Second's Ark | Test network (signet) only; Lightning in and out; receive and boarding from **Settings > Bark** |

## Asset Management

<img src="/assets/images/extension/08-dashboard.png" alt="KaleidoSwap dashboard with network status chips, the BTC balance card with Receive, Swap, and Send, and Your Assets with the filter toggle" style={{ maxWidth: "360px", width: "100%", height: "auto", display: "block", margin: "1.5rem auto" }} />

### Viewing Balances

The KaleidoSwap dashboard displays all your assets:

- **BTC balance** across Spark, Arkade, Liquid, Bitcoin L1, and RLN Lightning when connected
- **Spark assets** from the Spark account
- **Arkade assets** from the Arkade account
- **Liquid assets** from the Liquid account
- **RGB assets** from the node-less RGB-L1 account, or from RLN when connected
- **Protocol and network filters** for focusing on a single backing or network slice

Assets with the same ticker across backings are merged into a single dashboard row that expands into its per-network balances.

### Hiding Assets

You can hide assets you don't want on the dashboard from the **Manage asset list** button beside Your Assets. Hidden assets keep their balances; they are just removed from the list until you unhide them.

### Asset Details

Tap any asset to view details:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Ticker** | Short asset symbol (e.g., USDT) |
| **Name** | Full asset name |
| **Precision** | Decimal places |
| **Account** | Protocol account that owns the asset |
| **Spendable Balance** | Amount available for the selected route |
| **Network Badges** | Where the asset can be used, such as Spark, Arkade, Liquid, RGB-L1, or RGB-LN |

## Sending Payments

The KaleidoSwap Extension automatically detects the payment type based on what you paste or enter in the destination field.

### Supported Destination Formats

| Format | Detected As | Example |
|--------|------------|---------|
| **BOLT11 invoice** | Lightning payment | `lnbc1...` |
| **Lightning address** | Lightning payment | `name@example.com` |
| **LNURL-pay** | Lightning payment | `lnurl1...` |
| **BOLT12 offer** | Lightning payment | `lno1...` |
| **RGB invoice** | RGB asset transfer | `rgb:...` |
| **Bitcoin address** | On-chain payment | `bc1q...`, `3...`, `1...` |
| **Liquid address / URI** | Liquid transfer | `lq1...`, `liquidnetwork:...` |
| **Spark address** | Spark transfer | Spark address string |
| **Arkade route** | Arkade transfer or offboard route | Arkade-compatible destination |

<FeatureVideo name="address-auto-routing" title="Pasting any destination: the wallet picks the network" portrait />

### Send Flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter Destination">
    Paste, type, or scan (QR image) the destination address or invoice. The KaleidoSwap Extension auto-detects the payment type.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter Amount">
    For addresses, Lightning addresses, LNURL-pay and amountless invoices, enter the amount in sats, or tap the unit beside the field to switch to fiat. For invoices with an amount it is pre-filled. **Max** fills the spendable balance and, for on-chain sends, reserves the network fee. On-chain sends offer **Slow / Normal / Fast / Custom** fee rates.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review">
    Select **Review Payment** and check the payment details:
    - Destination
    - Amount
    - Selected route/account
    - Fee or route notes when available
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm">
    Select **Confirm & Send** to execute the payment. A status screen shows the result. If a Lightning payment is still pending after a few minutes, Send stays blocked for that destination; check **Activity** instead of paying again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<img src="/assets/images/extension/14-send-form.png" alt="Send screen with an on-chain destination detected, balance in sats, fee rate options, the route list, and the Review Payment button" style={{ maxWidth: "360px", width: "100%", height: "auto", display: "block", margin: "1.5rem auto" }} />

<Note>
Paying Lightning from Arkade uses an Arkade Intents swap. It pays **invoices only**: Lightning addresses and LNURL are refused. The review shows the solver and its fee before you confirm. On mainnet the default solver is Ark Labs (third party).
</Note>

<img src="/assets/images/extension/37-send-arkade-lightning-review.png" alt="Send review for a Lightning invoice paid from Arkade through an Arkade Intents swap, showing the solver and the solver fee" style={{ maxWidth: "360px", width: "100%", height: "auto", display: "block", margin: "1.5rem auto" }} />

### Amounts in Fiat

Amounts can be typed in fiat: tap the unit next to an amount field to switch between sats and fiat, or pick another currency. What is sent is always sats, and the converted figure follows the price. This is offered for BTC wherever the form asks for an amount.

### Payment Links

Clicking a `bitcoin:`, `lightning:` or `lnurl:` link on a web page opens the wallet on a pre-filled Send screen, or the LNURL-auth / LNURL-withdraw prompt.

### Receive Flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick an Asset">
    Tap **Receive** and choose **Bitcoin** (you pick the account next), one of **Your assets**, **Add an asset**, or **Bridge from another chain**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick a Route">
    Choose **By method** (how the sender pays: Bitcoin address, Lightning invoice, Spark transfer, Arkade transfer, Boarding, Liquid address) or **By account** (where the funds land). Each row shows the expected speed and fee.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set an Amount (Optional)">
    Leave it empty for any amount, or enter one in sats or, after tapping the unit, in your fiat currency.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Share">
    Copy the address or invoice, or show the QR code. Expired Lightning invoices are flagged and can be regenerated.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Account | Receive methods |
|---------|-----------------|
| **Spark** | Spark transfer, Lightning invoice, Bitcoin address (static deposit address that moves into Spark) |
| **Arkade** | Arkade transfer, Boarding (on-chain into Arkade), Lightning via an Arkade Intents swap |
| **Liquid** | Liquid address (L-BTC and Liquid assets) |
| **RGB (node-less)** | RGB invoice on Bitcoin L1 |
| **Lightning node** | Bitcoin address and Lightning invoice, including RGB assets over Lightning on an RGB Lightning node |

### RGB Invoices

The RGB form can lock the invoice to one asset or accept **Any RGB asset**, including assets the wallet doesn't hold yet. The amount is optional. Under **Advanced** you set the expiry and the receive mode: **Blinded** (with privacy; uses one of your spare UTXOs) or **Witness** (the sender adds BTC to fund the output). While the invoice is on screen, an incoming payment is detected automatically.

## Receive and Send

### Receive

Receive BTC or supported assets into the selected account:

1. Click **Receive** on the dashboard
2. Choose the asset and route
3. Copy the address or invoice, or show the QR code
4. Send funds from an external wallet or service

### Send

Send pays from the selected account to a detected destination:

1. Click **Send** on the dashboard
2. Paste a Bitcoin address, Lightning invoice, BOLT12 offer, Lightning address, LNURL, RGB invoice, Liquid address or `liquidnetwork:` URI, Spark address, or Arkade destination
3. Enter the amount
4. Select **Review Payment**, check the suggested route, then **Confirm & Send**

<img src="/assets/images/extension/16-withdraw-form.png" alt="Empty Send screen with the What you can send to panel listing Lightning, Bitcoin, Spark, Liquid, Arkade, RGB, and cross-chain USDC or USDT" style={{ maxWidth: "360px", width: "100%", height: "auto", display: "block", margin: "1.5rem auto" }} />

For sends from Spark to external chains (EVM networks, Solana, and more), see [Bridge](/extensions/kaleidoswap-extension/bridge).

### Transaction List

- Lists recent transactions across all connected accounts; sources load concurrently and render progressively
- Each entry shows: type, amount, status, carrier network, and timestamp
- Tabs for **All / In / Swap / Out**, a status filter (**All Status / Confirmed / Pending / Failed**), and network chips (All, On-chain, Lightning, Spark, Arkade, Liquid, depending on which accounts are active)

## Settings

Open **Settings** from the bottom navigation. The page is grouped into **Accounts**, **Payments**, **Security**, and **App**, with **Lock Wallet** and **Disconnect Accounts** at the bottom.

<img src="/assets/images/extension/11-settings.png" alt="Settings main page with the Accounts list at the top, followed by the Payments, Security, and App sections" style={{ maxWidth: "360px", width: "100%", height: "auto", display: "block", margin: "1.5rem auto" }} />

### Accounts

- **Spark** - Network selection, SDK details, and connection status
- **Arkade** - Network selection, VTXO settings, boarding flows, lifecycle settings, and an optional rotating-address (HD) mode with restore via gap scan
- **Arkade Intents** - Pay and receive Lightning from your Arkade balance through an RFQ solver. Configure it in **Settings > Arkade > Arkade Intents**. Sends and receives use the normal Send and Receive screens. The review shows the solver fee; stuck swaps can be refunded from the same settings page.
- **RGB** - Choose the backing: **Node-less** (on-device RGB with BTC on Bitcoin L1; network, Indexer URL, optional RGB transport endpoints) or **RLN node** (your own RGB Lightning Node over Direct or Wallet Connect). From here you also manage **UTXOs** (colorable, colored and normal UTXOs), **Issue asset** (mint a new asset from the wallet), and RGB **Backup**
- **Liquid** - Network selection and the Esplora/waterfalls sync endpoint (a fast default is provided; you can supply your own)
- **Bark** - Second's Ark network: BTC with Lightning in and out. Test network (signet) only
- **Lightning Node** - Connect one external Lightning node for deposits, withdrawals, and balance: **RGB Lightning node**, **LND** (REST HTTPS URL plus a restricted macaroon), or **Nostr Wallet Connect**. Core Lightning and LDK are shown as coming soon. Only one node is active at a time
- **Nostr** - Nostr Identity, Nostr Connect, and Wallet Connect

### KaleidoSwap Handle

You can claim a **Lightning address handle** (`yourname@kaleidoswap.me`) from **Settings > Payments > Lightning Address**. The handle needs Spark connected and points at your Spark address. It checks availability live as you type and is automatically re-linked when you restore the wallet. Use **Refresh target** if it falls out of sync, or **Release this handle** to pick a new name.

<img src="/assets/images/extension/20-handle-claim.png" alt="Claim your address screen with a name field, the kaleidoswap.me suffix, and a live availability check" style={{ maxWidth: "360px", width: "100%", height: "auto", display: "block", margin: "1.5rem auto" }} />

### Nostr

- **Settings > Nostr > Nostr Identity** - View your public key (npub), view/export the session private key when available, and import a key, or revert an imported key to the identity derived from your recovery phrase
- **Settings > Connected Apps** - Review every site and app connected to the wallet, including NIP-46 (bunker) clients, and revoke individual capabilities
- **Settings > Nostr > Nostr Connect** (NIP-46) - Generate a `bunker://` connection string so external Nostr apps can request signatures remotely
- **Settings > Nostr > Wallet Connect** - Pair external apps to the wallet over NWC with a QR code, and set amount-aware budgets

<img src="/assets/images/extension/39-settings-nostr.png" alt="Settings Nostr hub with the Nostr Identity, Nostr Connect, and Wallet Connect tiles" style={{ maxWidth: "360px", width: "100%", height: "auto", display: "block", margin: "1.5rem auto" }} />

<Warning>
Never share your Nostr private key (nsec). Anyone with this key can sign events and act as you.
</Warning>

### Security

**Settings > Security** is a sub-page that holds:

- **Change Password** - Update your wallet password
- **Passkey Login** - Register or remove a passkey (WebAuthn) for unlocking the wallet
- **Backup Wallet** - Reveal the recovery phrase after password confirmation, and create an RGB Lightning node backup file when RLN is connected. Node-less RGB backups live under **Settings > RGB > Backup**.
- **Auto-Lock Timer** - Set the inactivity timeout, from 1 minute to 1 hour, or never
- **Chrome Sync note** - Explains what Chrome Sync may copy and how to turn off extension sync

<img src="/assets/images/extension/23-backup-wallet.png" alt="Backup Wallet screen requiring the password before decrypting the recovery phrase, with a warning and a summary of what the backup covers" style={{ maxWidth: "360px", width: "100%", height: "auto", display: "block", margin: "1.5rem auto" }} />

### App

Display preferences are inline pickers on the main Settings page:

| Setting | Options |
|---------|---------|
| **Bitcoin Unit** | Sats, BTC, or ₿ only |
| **Fiat Currency** | USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, CHF, CAD, CNY, KRW |
| **Language** | English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Japanese, Italian, Korean, Chinese |

The App section also holds **Open in Full Tab**, **Send Feedback** (report a problem, optionally appending redacted diagnostics or downloading them), and **About**. The auto-lock timeout lives under **Settings > Security**.

### Full-Tab View

The wallet normally opens in the browser side panel (or a toolbar popup). For a larger workspace, use **Settings > Open in Full Tab** to open the wallet as a full browser tab.
