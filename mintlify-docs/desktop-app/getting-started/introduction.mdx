The KaleidoSwap Desktop App is a full-featured application for trading Bitcoin and RGB assets on the Lightning Network. It gives you complete control over your node, wallet, and channels.

## What the Desktop App Covers

| Area | What the Desktop App gives you |
|------|-------------------------------|
| **Node control** | Run an RGB Lightning Node on your machine, or point the app at a remote one you host |
| **Wallet** | Create and unlock accounts, generate or restore a recovery phrase, hold BTC and RGB balances |
| **Channels** | Open a channel with a peer directly, or order one from an LSP with RGB assets pre-allocated |
| **Trading** | Request quotes and settle atomic swaps between BTC and RGB assets from your own node |
| **Backups** | Export channel backups so channel funds stay recoverable after data loss |
| **Settings** | Node endpoint, RGB proxy, and network configuration, per account |

## How It Works

The Desktop App is a client, not a custodian. It drives an **RGB Lightning Node** that holds your keys, manages channel state, and validates RGB contracts locally.

- The **app** is the interface: accounts, balances, channels, and trading screens
- The **RGB Lightning Node** holds the keys and the channel state, whether it runs on your machine or on a server you control
- **LSPs** supply inbound liquidity and act as the counterparty that locks the other leg of a swap
- **Bitcoin** is where channels anchor and where positions finally settle

No third party has access to your funds at any point. For how this fits the wider stack, see [Architecture](/whats-kaleidoswap/architecture#client-architecture).

## Everyday Flow

1. **Unlock** — enter your password; the app starts or reconnects to your node
2. **Review balances** — on-chain BTC, Lightning balance, and RGB assets for the selected account
3. **Move funds** — deposit, withdraw, or open a channel
4. **Trade** — quote a pair and settle an atomic swap against the maker

## System Requirements

- **macOS** 12+ (Apple Silicon and Intel)
- **Windows** 10+ (64-bit)
- **Linux** (Ubuntu 20.04+, Debian 11+, or equivalent)

For building from source, you'll also need Rust, Node.js, and the Tauri prerequisites.
