title: Withdrawals
description: Step-by-step instructions for withdrawing funds from your wallet in the Kaleidoswap Desktop App
---

Withdrawing funds from your wallet allows you to send Bitcoin or RGB assets to other addresses, according to the following process:


1. **Navigate to "Channels"**: Click on the "Withdraw" button on the left sidebar.
2. **Withdrawal Method**: You will authomatically select the withdrawal method by pasting a Bitcoin onchain invoice, a Lightning invoice / address or a RGB invoice.
3. **Enter Amount**: Specify the amount of Bitcoin or RGB asset to be withdrawn.
4. **Fee Rate**: slow, normal or fast for onchain transaction.
5. **Review Transaction**: Double-check the details.
8. **Confirm and Send**: Click "Withdraw" to initiate the transaction.

![withdraw-menu](/images/desktop-app/withdraw-menu.png)

**Differences between BTC and RGB onchain withdrawal**: The steps to withdraw generic assets are almost identical to those required to withdraw BTC with the only difference being that onchain you will need to specify the `blinded UTxO` instead of the address.
