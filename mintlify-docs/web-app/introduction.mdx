## Key Features

### Order-Based Trading

KaleidoSwap uses a **taker-maker model** for swaps. You create an order, pay the deposit, and the maker executes the swap by sending the asset to your recipient address. This is not an atomic swap -- you pay first and then wait for the maker to fulfill your order.

<Note>
In this phase, swaps are **not atomic**. After you pay, the maker processes your order and sends the swapped asset to the recipient address you provided.
</Note>

### Multi-Wallet Support

Connect your preferred Bitcoin wallet:
- **Alby** - Lightning-native browser wallet
- **Xverse** - Bitcoin Web3 wallet
- **BitMask** - RGB & Lightning wallet
- **KaleidoSwap Extension** - Bitcoin Layer 2 extension with Spark, Arkade, RGB, and DApp support

### Protocol Support

Trade across different protocols and layers:
- **RGB on Lightning** - Instant RGB asset swaps
- **RGB On-chain** - On-chain RGB settlements
- **Bitcoin Lightning** - Native BTC swaps

### Real-Time Market Data

- Live quotes with automatic refresh
- USD price equivalents
- Fee estimation before order creation
- Min/max amount validation

## Interface Overview

The trading interface provides:

1. **From Card** - Select the asset you want to swap from
2. **To Card** - Select the asset you want to receive
3. **Recipient Address** - Enter the address where you want to receive the swapped asset
4. **Network Selection** - Choose the protocol layer
5. **Quote Display** - Real-time exchange rate and fees
6. **Swap Button** - Create the swap order

After creating an order, you are redirected to the **Order Page** where you can view deposit instructions, pay via your wallet or manually, and track the order status in real time.

## Security

- **Non-custodial** - You control your keys
- **Order-based model** - You pay first, then the maker fulfills the order. In this phase, swaps rely on the maker to send the asset after receiving your payment.
- **No account needed** - Just connect your wallet
- **SSL encrypted** - All connections are secure
- **Transparent tracking** - Monitor every order in real time on the order page
