## Phases

The [`KaleidoSwap` API](/sdk/kaleidoswap) reports one set of phases for every swap, and acts on them for you.

| Phase | What happened | Your app |
|---|---|---|
| `created` / `awaiting_funding` | The swap exists and is saved | Pay the invoice, or fund the lockup |
| `funded` | Your payment or lockup was seen | Wait |
| `locked` | The on-chain lockup is in place | Wait |
| `claiming` | The claim is being broadcast and confirmed | Wait |
| `settled` | Final: the swap completed | Done |
| `refundable` | A swap you funded failed; your lockup can be recovered | Wait: the SDK refunds it, with the maker's signature or after the timeout |
| `refunding` | The refund is being broadcast and confirmed | Wait |
| `refunded` | Final: your funds came back | Done |
| `failed` | Final: the swap ended without settling | Start a new swap |

A swap moves only while your app follows it or runs `resume()`. The maker's own status, described in the rest of this page, stays readable as `swap.record().rawStatus`.

The tables below are the maker's statuses, which you act on yourself when you use the swap engine.

## On-chain → Lightning (submarine)

| # | Status | What happened | Your app |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `swap.created` / `invoice.set` | The swap exists with your invoice | Fund `address` with exactly `expectedAmount` |
| 2 | `transaction.mempool` | Your lockup is in the mempool | Wait |
| 3 | `transaction.confirmed` | Your lockup confirmed | Wait |
| 4 | `invoice.pending` | The maker is paying your invoice | Wait |
| 5 | `invoice.paid` | Your invoice is paid | Nothing more to do |
| 6 | `transaction.claim.pending` / `transaction.claimed` | The maker claimed your lockup | Done |

Failure states: `invoice.failedToPay`, `transaction.lockupFailed` (wrong amount), `swap.expired`. In each case, **refund your lockup**.

## Lightning → on-chain (reverse)

| # | Status | What happened | Your app |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `swap.created` | The swap exists | Pay `invoice` |
| 2 | `transaction.mempool` | The maker's lockup is in the mempool | Wait. A claim on an unconfirmed lockup needs `allowZeroConf: true` |
| 3 | `transaction.confirmed` | The maker's lockup confirmed | **Claim it** |
| 4 | `invoice.settled` | Your claim revealed the preimage and the maker settled your payment | Done |

Failure states: `invoice.expired` and `swap.expired` (nothing was paid), `transaction.failed` and `transaction.refunded` (the maker's lockup failed or was refunded; your Lightning payment is released).

## Chain → chain

| # | Status | What happened | Your app |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `swap.created` | The swap exists | Fund `lockupDetails.lockupAddress` |
| 2 | `transaction.mempool` / `transaction.confirmed` | Your lockup is seen, then confirmed | Wait |
| 3 | `transaction.server.mempool` / `transaction.server.confirmed` | The maker locked on the destination chain | **Claim it** |
| 4 | `transaction.claimed` | Done | Done |

Failure states: `transaction.lockupFailed`, `swap.expired`, `transaction.failed`, `transaction.refunded`. **Refund your lockup.** If you locked a different amount than quoted, the maker may offer a re-quote instead; see [Move funds between chains](/sdk/chain-swaps#accept-a-re-quote).

## Deadlines

Every lockup has a `timeoutBlockHeight`.

- **Claims must confirm before it.** After the deadline, the other side can refund. The SDK's claim helpers stop 6 blocks early on Bitcoin and 60 on Liquid by default.
- **Refunds through the script path open at it.** Before it, a refund needs the maker to co-sign.
- **Chain swaps have two deadlines:** the source chain's for your refund, the destination chain's for your claim.

Your app must be running to claim. Check open swaps on start, on resume, and on a schedule.

## Following a swap

### WebSocket

<CodeGroup>
```typescript TypeScript
import { SwapWsApi } from "@kaleidorg/swap-sdk";

const ws = new SwapWsApi("wss://maker.signet.kaleidoswap.com/v2/ws");
void ws.runWsLoop();                 // runs in the background; do not await
const updates = ws.updates();        // take the stream before subscribing
await ws.subscribeSwap(swap.id);

for (;;) {
  const { id, status } = await updates.next();
  console.log(id, status);
  if (status === "invoice.settled") break;
}
ws.close();
```

```python Python
ws = client.ws()
asyncio.create_task(ws.run_ws_loop())
updates = ws.updates()               # take the stream before subscribing
await ws.subscribe_swap(swap.id)

while True:
    update = await updates.next()
    print(update.id, update.status)
    if update.status == "invoice.settled":
        break
await ws.shutdown()
```
</CodeGroup>

A dead socket reports no error, which happens on mobile after the app is backgrounded. Check `isConnected()` and fall back to polling.

### Polling

`client.swap(id)` (`get_swap` in Python) returns the current status. Poll it on start, on resume, and after a reconnect.

<Note>
A status is the maker's report, not proof. Before you claim, check the lockup on your own chain backend. Before you refund, check the chain tip against the deadline. See [Trust model](/sdk/trust-model).
</Note>

Lost your local records? See [Keys and recovery](/sdk/keys-and-recovery#restoring-swaps).
